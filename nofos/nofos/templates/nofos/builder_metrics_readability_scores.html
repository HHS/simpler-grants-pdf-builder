How saved readability metrics are preserved

Saved checkpoints stay listed when a NOFO is archived, and when the person who saved them is deleted. Deleting a NOFO deletes its saved checkpoints.

Unlike the usage & quality metrics, this page uses each NOFO’s current group, not its group when a checkpoint was saved. Internal and staging are excluded: NOFOs in the Bloomworks or staging group are not listed. Moving a NOFO into either group removes it from this page, and moving it out lists it again with all of its saved checkpoints.

There is no automatic age-based cutoff for saved checkpoints. This history is separate from application logs and database backups.
