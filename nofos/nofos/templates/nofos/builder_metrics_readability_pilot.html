About these metrics

Figures cover currently retained outcomes, not lifetime usage. Rates include all recorded attempts, including disabled and busy responses. CSRF, authentication, permission and upstream rejections are not counted.

Source describes the upload route, not whether a visitor happened to be signed in. Unknown records have no established source. OpDiv filtering isn't available: outcomes have no document or user association. Internal pilot participants are included.

Chart windows are the last 30 days and 12 weeks. Missing observations show “No data,” not zero: recording may have been off or retention may have removed records. The current day and week are in progress. Weeks start Monday; dates use the application time zone.
