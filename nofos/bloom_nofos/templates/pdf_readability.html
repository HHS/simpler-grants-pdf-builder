How to read these results

These are estimates, not a compliance, accessibility, or clearance determination. NOFO Builder measures its own drafts from structured HTML; this report measures text extracted from a PDF. Scores can differ even for the same document.

PDF requirements

Use a text-based HHS NOFO PDF. Keep the opportunity number, Assistance Listing number, and Grants.gov reference on the first two pages when possible.

You can check an in-progress draft; it does not need to be designed for publication. Export a copy containing the writing you want to measure. Writer-only notes and template prompts may affect the results. Blank templates without identifying details may not be recognized.

Scanned or image-only pages need OCR before analysis. Untagged PDFs can receive a report, but their estimates have lower reliability.
