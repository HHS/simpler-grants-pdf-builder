Get registered

SAM.gov

Your organization must have an active account with SAM.gov to apply unless you are exempt under 2 CFR 25. SAM.gov registration can take several weeks.

To register:

  • Go to SAM.gov Entity Registration and select Get Started. On the same page, you can also find the Entity Registration Checklist for the information you will need to register.
  • Your organization must agree to the financial assistance general certifications and representations specifically. The certifications for contracts are different.

When you register, your organization will also receive its required Unique Entity Identifier (UEI).

Once you register:

  • Your organization will have to maintain its registration throughout the life of any award.
  • If your organization has multiple UEIs, use the one associated with your physical location.

Grants.gov

To apply for this opportunity, your organization and the people who will work on your application must have an active account with Grants.gov. You can follow the step-by-step instructions at the Grants.gov Quick Start Guide for Applicants.

We recommend that you register and set up your organization’s account at least four weeks before the application deadline.
