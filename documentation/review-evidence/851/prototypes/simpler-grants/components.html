Summary

Link to this section: Summary

This program funds schools of nursing to recruit, prepare and keep nurse faculty. There are two application rounds; apply in the round that fits your timeline.

Note for current recipients: If you have an active award under this program, apply in the second round only.

Eligible applicants

Link to this section: Eligible applicants

You may apply if you are one of the following:

  1. An accredited school of nursing.
  2. A health care facility that partners with a school of nursing.
  3. A state or local government that funds nurse education, including:
    1. State boards of nursing.
    2. Workforce development agencies.

Applicants in category (c)(ii) must include a letter of support from a school of nursing.
