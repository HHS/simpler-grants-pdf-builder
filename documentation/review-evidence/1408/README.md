# Applicant response tables: illustrated problem

Supporting evidence for [#1408](https://github.com/HHS/simpler-grants-pdf-builder/issues/1408).

- [Scoring question with a missing response grid](b1-missing-response-grid.png)
- [Two applicant response shapes](b1-required-response-shapes.png)

These are browser screenshots of synthetic reconstructions, not screenshots of
internal Word drafts or Builder output. They show the source-document gap and
abbreviated examples of the information applicants are expected to supply.
They do not prescribe a layout or establish an import or PDF defect.
